# Sample Questions

You can ask the Concierge anything about being a soccer official in Oregon. Here are some example questions to get started.

## Getting started

* I'm a new ref. Where do I start?
* How do I get assignments in Oregon?
* What do I need to do to referee in Reftown?

## License and certification

* Check my license status
* Look up my USSF certification
* When does my referee license expire?

## Rules and procedures

* What is the offside rule?
* How do I report a red card?
* What are the substitution rules for OYSA?
* Where do I submit game reports for [league name]?

## Assignments and leagues

* Why am I not getting assignments?
* How do I set my availability in Reftown?
* Which organizations assign games in the Portland area?

Try typing any of these in the chat, or ask your own question. Use **Look up License Info** in the menu to check your USSF license status.
