
This directory lists Oregon organizations (and similar entities) that assign and pay referees.

**How to use:** Join the RefTown organization (or parent org for payor leagues), then set availability. Assignor and hotline info is needed for game-day issues and reporting.

## List of NWSC Leagues

**NorthWest Soccer Central (NWSC)** is a consolidated "umbrella" organization. Users registered with NWSC can access several sub-groups (referred to as **Payor Leagues**) which are *not* always listed as separate top-level Reftown organizations in the directory.
To access these, a referee must register with **NWSC** and then "Request to Join" the specific Payor League from their profile.
