## How to sign up for games

Referees are assigned through NorthWest Soccer Central (NWSC). Registering for NWSC in RefTown covers all NWSC payor leagues.

- **RefTown link:** Use the NWSC organization in RefTown (https://reftown.com/default.asp?Assoc=NWSC--555).
- **Steps:** Join NWSC in RefTown using this link: https://reftown.com/registration.asp?RegType=Official&AssocRID=555&Existing=-1, then from your profile request to join the payor league "S5 - OCSC". Set availability for assignments and look for games to request from the main page.
