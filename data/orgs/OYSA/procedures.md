## OYSA Game Day Procedures

Both teams are required to bring an official, printed roster.  Each participating player must appear on the roster along with their player ID number.  For some competitions players may be written in on the roster, subject to certain restrictions in the league rules of competition.  Make sure you understand those restrictions.

If one team neglects to bring a roster, the roster from the other team may be used, since the printed rosters include both teams and should be identical.

Unofficial rosters are unacceptable.  If neither team has the official gameday roster, do not start the game, call the game day emergency hotline.

Use a line up and roll call to check players off the roster.  Use this time also for an equipment check.  As each player is called verify their correct jersey number is on the roster and make any necessary corrections.  For OYSA competitive league, you will also need to check the participants and coaches OYSA member cards.  Developmental League does not require players or coaches to show player cards if they are on the printed roster.

The only adults allowed on the team sidelines are the coaches on the printed rosters.  Other adults must move to the spectator side.

**Important:** The rosters contain further instructions on what to do with the match rosters after the game, and they also contain the gameday hotline number.  If you have any questions, call that number.
