# OYSA Developmental Academy

Also referred to as "development league", OYSA Developmental is a great opportunity for new and young referees to start out.  This covers the U8-U10 age groups, typically 1st to 3rd grade.

There are several Developmental leagues in Oregon.
* The main one in Portland is OYSA Developmental League.
* Valley Academy hosts games at Capital FC Soccer Complex in Salem.
* Capitol Futbol Club (CFC) hosts CFC Junior Academy.
* Bend FC Junior Development Academy hosted by Bend FC.

* REFEREE PAY – A great perk of being a soccer official is the pay! Development Academy League (DL) pay for a solo center is $35 per game.  Once you gain some experience, you can take on more demanding assignments within OYSA – and with that comes even higher rates.  See our PayScale in RefTown for more details.
* REFEREE MENTOR SUPPORT – OYSA DL offers mentor support to all our referees throughout the spring season for league games.  Our experienced staff is dedicated to creating a fun, supportive, learning environment for all referees to enjoy and to have a memorable experience.  When you are out on the fields and have a question or need assistance, simply look for a mentor and we’d be happy to help!      
* DEVELOPMENTAL PATHWAY – OYSA offers a pathway for developing our valued referees.  From your first game as an AR or learning how to become a solo Center in our amazing Development Academy League (DL), to working with a three-person crew in our competitive league, we’re your destination!  Also, if you are new to refereeing in the DL and need a mentor, we can provide one for you.  Whatever your personal goals may be, we can help you achieve them!
* DEDICATED ASSIGNORS – We’re always just an email away!  If you require assistance with getting assigned to games, shoot us an email. You will have easy access to assignors who are fully invested in your continued referee development.   Whatever your needs, we’re here for YOU!  So please reach out anytime, and we’d be more than happy to help.
* FIELD LOCATIONS SPRING 2026 – Sunday DL games are typically hosted at Wilsonville HS, Delta Park, and Sherwood MS. Regardless of where you live, please consider refereeing Sundays in our OYSA Development Academy League (DL).  It’s an exciting atmosphere loaded with fun and plenty of soccer to go around.
* GAME TIMES & DATES – Games typically kick off as early as 8:00am and end sometime between 6-8pm every Sunday.  The spring 2026 league should begin Sunday, February 22nd, and will run for eight to nine weeks with time-off for spring break.  Check out the OYSA website for more details. 
* REFEREE QUALITIES – OYSA looks for aspiring referees who have the potential to develop the Eight Qualities of a Great Official: Integrity, Hustle, Judgement, Communication, Consistency, Courage, Common Sense, & Commitment.  OYSA fully values our referees!  We would like to help our officials develop these qualities to be the best they can be, both on the soccer field and in life!

If you are interested in refereeing youth soccer with us on Sundays and wish to grow in a supportive and fun environment, please consider joining our OYSA Referee Family.  We truly do value and appreciate our officials and strive every day to live up to that commitment.
