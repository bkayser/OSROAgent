# Officials Onboarding Guide


Overview

Ref Insight provides a centralized platform that streamlines all aspects of your officiating experience. As a sports official, you'll benefit from:

- Simplified assignment management that matches your qualifications and availability

- Availability management tools to help balance your officiating with other commitments

- Streamlined match reporting for quick and thorough documentation

- Secure payment processing that gets you paid faster

The platform is designed to be easy to use and efficient so you can focus on what matters most: officiating at your best.

Centralized Platform

A single hub for all your officiating duties, accessible across devices

Multi-Sport Support

Primarily focused on soccer with expanding support for basketball, baseball, and other sports

All Levels

Suitable for officiating from youth recreational leagues to professional competitions

The platform adapts to your specific officiating needs while maintaining consistent functionality across all sports and levels.

Account Activation: Getting Started

Invitation Receipt

An email with a unique activation link will be sent to you by your assignor or association administrator.

Age Verification

Submit your date of birth for age verification. Users under 18 require parental consent, which is captured securely via email during activation.

Profile Completion

Complete your profile by providing required information (name, email, address, phone number) and payment details (bank info).

The activation process is quick, typically taking less than 10 minutes. Rest assured, your information remains secure and is only shared with authorized administrators.

Considerations for Minor Officials (under 18)

- Parental/guardian consent is required for account activation, secured via an emailed link.

- Enhanced privacy protections.

- Specialized assignment opportunities tailored for youth officials.

- All system notifications are sent to parents or guardians for visibility and SafeSport compliance.

Ref Insight ensures an age-appropriate experience, offering comprehensive tools to support officials at every stage of their development.

Availability Management: Two Models

Ref Insight offers two complementary approaches to managing your availability.

You can switch between models at any time based on your schedule and preferences.

Add Blockouts

In this model, you are presumed available for assignments by default. You will then block out specific dates or times when you are unavailable. This approach is best for officials with generally open schedules or those who prefer to proactively manage their commitments.

**Add Available Dates/Times**

Here, you are presumed unavailable by default. You must actively mark specific dates or times when you are available for assignments. This model is ideal for officials with very busy or unpredictable schedules, or those who prefer to only accept assignments on an ad-hoc basis.

Add Blockouts

How It Works

With this model, the system considers you available for assignments by default. You only need to indicate when you are **not** available.

Best For:

- Officials with predictable schedules

- Those who officiate frequently

- People with few conflicts

Key Features:

- Block off unavailable dates with a single click

- Set recurring unavailability patterns

- Create blockout periods for vacations, school, or work

**Add Available Dates/Times**

How It Works

With this model, the system considers you unavailable by default. You must explicitly mark the dates or times when you **are** available for assignments.

Best For:

- Officials with irregular schedules

- Those who officiate occasionally

- People juggling multiple commitments

Key Features:

- Designate specific time slots for availability

- Set recurring availability patterns

- Precise control over assignment opportunities

Key Availability Features

Flexible Model Switching

Toggle between "Add Blockouts" and "Add Available Dates/Times" models with a single click. The system will automatically convert your existing availability settings.

Granular Time Selection

Set availability down to 15-minute increments for precise scheduling control. Perfect for officials who can work partial days or specific time windows.

Real-Time Updates

Changes to your availability are instantly reflected in the system, ensuring assignors always see your current availability status when making assignments.

Assignment Management

Ref Insight offers two primary methods for receiving officiating assignments:

Assignor

Personally selected by your assignor based on your qualifications, performance history, and availability. These assignments require a response (accept or decline).

Self-Assigned

Open to all qualified officials on a first-come, first-served basis. These self-assignment opportunities allow you to build your schedule independently.

Self-Assignment Criteria

Role-Based Eligibility

Ref Insight intelligently filters assignments based on your registered location, age, and officiating experience, ensuring you only see opportunities that align with your profile.

Geographic Restrictions

Assignments are geographically restricted by your self-defined travel radius. The platform precisely calculates travel distance from your home address to optimize matching with nearby venues.

Other Considerations

The assignor can limit the number of matches that can be self-assigned in a given day. They may also set these assignments to accepted or tentative status.

This robust self-assignment system ensures a fair distribution of opportunities by precisely matching qualified officials with appropriate games. New officials will typically begin with entry-level assignments, progressing to more advanced roles as they gain experience and demonstrate consistent performance.

Ref Insight Pay

Ref Insight Pay simplifies the payment process for officials, ensuring timely and secure transactions. It processes payments securely through its banking partners, utilizing end-to-end encryption. You will receive email notifications when payments are initiated and completed.

Comprehensive Payment History

A complete payment history is accessible within the app, allowing you to track all your earnings and transactions.

Flexible Delivery Options

Payors determine the payment delivery speed, with standard delivery taking 3-4 business days. Payments can be received via ACH (direct deposit) or check by mail, depending on the payor's chosen method.

Off-System Payment Tracking

While some payors may opt for payment outside the system, documentation for these payments is available within Ref Insight if assignors choose to input the information, ensuring all your earnings are accounted for.

Payment Information Setup

One-Time Setup Process

Ref Insight uses a centralized payment system that works across all leagues and assignors using the platform. Complete the setup once, and you'll be able to receive payments from any organization using Ref Insight.

Required Information:

- Bank routing number

- Account number

- Account type (checking/savings), must accept ACH

- Social Security Number or Tax ID

- Legal Name

**Tax Information**

Ref Insight provides 1099 data to payors for officials who earn more than $600 in a calendar year from a single organization. Your SSN or Tax ID is required for this reporting.

**Security Note**

All banking information is encrypted using bank-grade security protocols. Ref Insight never stores complete banking details on its servers.

Notification System

You can customize notification preferences in your account settings, choosing which alerts you receive through each channel. Time-critical notifications will always be sent through all available methods.

Best Practices for Officials

Assignment Responsiveness

Respond promptly to assignment offers (within 24-48 hours). Timely responses improve your reliability rating and lead to more assignment opportunities.

Reporting Accuracy

Submit complete and objective match reports. Accurate documentation protects all participants and maintains the integrity of competition records.

Availability Management

Keep your availability calendar current. Last-minute changes impact assignors' ability to cover games appropriately and may affect your future opportunities.

Professional Communication

Maintain respectful, clear communication with assignors, fellow officials, and administrators through platform channels.
