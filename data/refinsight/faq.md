# Frequently Asked Questions | Ref Insight

## General Questions

### What is Ref Insight

Ref Insight is a comprehensive platform for sports officiating management that streamlines scheduling, payments, and communication between officials, assignors, and sports organizations.

## Platform & Access

### How do users access Ref Insight

Users can access Ref Insight through our web platform on any device with an internet connection. Officials also have access to our dedicated mobile app available for both iOS and Android devices.


### How do officials get started with Ref Insight

Officials receive an activation email when added to the system. They can then set up their profile, input availability, and download the mobile app to stay connected.

## Officials Management

### How does Ref Insight handle officials who work for multiple organizations

Officials maintain a unified global profile that works across all organizations using Ref Insight, eliminating duplicate data entry and ensuring consistent information.

## Assigning

### How does the assignment process work

Assignors use our intuitive drag-and-drop interface to match officials to games. The system considers factors like availability, certification level, distance to venue, and other customizable criteria.

### Can multiple people handle assignments within one organization

Yes, our Flexible Assignor Permissions allow you to set up multiple assignors with customized permissions to distribute workload while maintaining appropriate access controls.

### Can officials self-assign or request specific games

Yes, you can enable Self-Assignment Capabilities that allow officials to request or self-assign to specific games based on your predetermined parameters, reducing administrative workload.

## Payments

### How does the payment process work

Ref Insight Pay, our comprehensive electronic payment system, allows organizations to process payments securely to officials, mentors, coaches, and staff. The system streamlines tax documentation and maintains clear transaction records.

### What payment methods are available

Payors can choose from various ACH speeds (standard, expedited, next day, same day), checks, wires, and other electronic transfer options. You can also track external payments made via cash or other payment apps.

### Are there age restrictions for receiving electronic payments

No, our Simple Payment Setup allows officials of all ages, including minors, to easily set up electronic payments by entering their routing and account numbers without complex verification processes.

### How does Ref Insight handle tax documentation

Ref Insight Pay partners with Routable to provide electronic W9 collection, allowing payors to gather tax forms in bulk from officials. Our system also helps identify 1099-eligible payees and provides comprehensive payment reporting for tax purposes. Routable can also provide assistance with filing upon request (additional fees apply).

## Communication

### How do officials receive notifications about assignments

Officials receive real-time updates through both email and push notifications to their mobile devices. They also have access to in-app messaging and notification history.

### Can officials communicate with their crew

Yes, officials can send messages to their crew, their assignor, or both directly from within the assignment in the app.

### Does Ref Insight support calendar integration

Yes, officials can sync their assignments to their preferred calendar application and share their schedules with others, such as parents/guardians, spouses, or family members.


## Development & Evaluation

### How does Ref Insight support official development

Our Scouting & Development tools allow organizations to create customizable observation forms, track engagement in training events, measure performance, and document progression through development tiers.

### Can we evaluate officials within the system

Yes, our Evaluation tools help assess official performance with structured observation forms that identify strengths, highlight growth areas, and track improvement over time. Sport-specific evaluation forms can be created upon request (additional fees may apply for custom evaluation forms). For U.S. Soccer-affiliated officials, Ref Insight will soon integrate with the RefEval online evaluation platform.

### Is video integration available for evaluations

We're developing integration with TeamXStream that will allow evaluators to link specific evaluation comments to match video clips, providing officials with visual references for feedback points.

### How does Ref Insight integrate with U.S. Soccer for evaluations

We're developing API integration with U.S. Soccer's evaluation platform to allow seamless data sharing for officials under their governance. Similar integrations with other National Governing Bodies can be made upon request (additional fees may apply).


### Is Ref Insight mobile-friendly

Yes, officials have access to dedicated iOS and Android apps, while all other interfaces are fully responsive and work on any device with an internet connection.
