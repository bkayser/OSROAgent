# Oregon Referee Landscape

This document describes how leagues, tournaments, and assigning work for Oregon soccer referees.

## Organizations and assigning

Referees work in multiple leagues and tournaments. Each league or tournament is managed by an **organization**. The organization might be a sanctioned USSF regional authority (e.g., Oregon Youth Soccer / OYSA) or a club (e.g., Oregon City Soccer Club, Eastside Timbers).

Most organizations have a single assignor but OYSA league games are spread across Oregon and southern Washington and use local referee assignors in each area.

Organizations that use **RefTown.com** manage referee assignments through that web app. Referees create an **availability schedule** in RefTown that is shared across organizations and payor leagues; assignors use it to assign games.

Some assignors in Oregon use other platforms, such as [StackOfficials](https://stackofficials.com/auth/login), [Arbiter Sports](https://app.arbitersports.com] or [RefInsight](https://refinsight.com) for assigning.  As you reach out to organizations for assignments, you may be directed to those platforms.  Those systems are not covered here.

To see the list of games available for requesting assignments or claiming assignments, use this reftown link: https://reftown.com/games.asp?openonly=1&AltSort=Date%2FLocation%2FTime&openonlyigav=1.  You can also see games available for request in reftown on the organizations main page, in the left under "Notices".  If there are open games in your organization they will appear under the heading "Open Games" or "Open Matches".

## RefTown: top-level organizations vs payor leagues

In RefTown there are two ways an organization can appear:

- **Top-level organization** – You join the org directly in RefTown. Example: Portland Youth Soccer Organization (PYSO). To get games with PYSO, follow RefTown instructions for joining that org.

- **Payor league under NWSC** – NorthWest Soccer Central (NWSC) is a RefTown organization that contains several **payor leagues** (e.g., Oregon City Soccer Club, and about five others). To get games with a payor league such as Oregon City Soccer Club (OCSC), you join **NWSC** in RefTown, then select the **OCSC payor league** from the list of payor leagues.

If you work for more than one organization or tournament, you must register for **each one** in RefTown (or on the platform that org uses).

## Leagues and Tournaments

Each league or tournament has its own:

- **Rules of competition** or **League Rules** (often as a PDF)
- **Referee pay scale or pay table**
- **Assignor** with contact information; there may be more than one

Additionally the competition may provide referees with:

- **Referee Procedures** (game report submission instructions, misconduct reporting, check-in guidance)
- **League or Tournament Hotline** for admins, coaches and issues about rosters and eligibility
- **Game day emergency hotline** (for no-shows, send-offs, injuries, etc.)

## How to find the assignor

A referee can find the assignor named in their reftown assignment details, or from the Contact List in the Reftown organization, available under the Main menu.
